## What I am joining

I am an adult who has been personally invited to a small, closed pilot of an experimental community-coordination application. Participation is voluntary. I may decline to join, skip any activity, stop using the application, or withdraw from the pilot without penalty.

The pilot is intended to test whether trusted neighbors can coordinate household status, practical requests, offers, and limited sharing of necessary household information before, during, and after disruptive events. It is not a public emergency-warning service.

## Experimental limits and non-reliance

I understand that the application is unfinished and may fail. Incident information or notifications may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, corrected, stale, inaccurate, unavailable, or never delivered because of source, network, device, software, polling, permission, or human failure.

I will not rely on Lee Hill Labs to discover, verify, report, interpret, monitor, or respond to an emergency. No LHL notification means only that LHL has no qualifying notification to show me; it does not mean there is no incident or that any person, property, road, route, or area is safe.

LHL does not continuously monitor me, my household, my property, or every incident. It does not provide emergency dispatch, evacuation authority, route guidance, fire perimeters, medical advice, or a verified declaration that anyone is safe.

## Emergencies, 911, and official instructions

My safety and official instructions come first. If I believe I am in danger or there is an emergency, I will move to safety and call 911 immediately when I am able rather than waiting for or using LHL. I will follow instructions from public authorities and official alerting channels.

No app-level `Call 911` or `Text 911` feature is approved for this pilot. LHL does not automatically contact 911, send an emergency report, confirm that emergency services were notified, or transmit my saved address, device location, identity, or incident details to emergency services. Submitting a request, acknowledgement, check-in, message, call, or text through another LHL feature does not contact 911 or report an emergency.

LHL's member-profile `Call` and `Text` actions contact the selected neighbor through the device's native applications. They are not 911 functions, and LHL does not guarantee that any call, text, or notification will be delivered.

## Live incidents and drills

The pilot may display information about a real wildfire or other approved incident. Any use during a live incident is voluntary observation and testing, not reliance for warning or response decisions. John Elliott, as LHL CEO and pilot owner, may continue, suspend, or end pilot operation. If John is unavailable or affected, a pre-designated alternate may suspend it under the approved pilot procedure.

The pilot is planned to include one fictional-event exercise to test conditions the community hopes never occur. Simulation content will be clearly labeled `TEST`, `DRILL`, or `SIMULATION`. I will receive advance notice of the planned drill date and time. A drill will not ask me to enter unsafe conditions, physically evacuate, disobey officials, or treat LHL as an authority. A real emergency or materially unsafe condition takes priority over the drill.

## Risks I acknowledge

I understand that:

- acting on missing, delayed, wrong, stale, misunderstood, or incomplete information can create risk;
- other participants' household status, incident check-ins, acknowledgements, requests, offers, messages, and availability are self-reported and may be incomplete or wrong;
- a check-in is not an independently verified statement that a person or household is safe;
- a displayed point distance is not a fire boundary, evacuation area, threat assessment, spread forecast, or safe-route recommendation;
- another participant may not respond, may withdraw an offer, or may be unable to help safely;
- I remain responsible for deciding whether I can safely offer or accept neighbor assistance, and LHL will not direct me into hazardous conditions; and
- participation may involve privacy, communication, technical, and ordinary interpersonal risks even with the safeguards described below.

This acknowledgment records my understanding of the pilot's risks and limits. It does not waive rights or create a release beyond any separate language later reviewed and approved by counsel.

## Information used in the pilot

The pilot may collect and use only the approved information needed to operate and evaluate it, which may include:

- my name, phone number, account and Boulder Heights membership state;
- my saved home address and an operator-verified coordinate derived from it for incident relevance;
- household profile and assistance information that I choose to provide and am authorized to provide about members of my household;
- voluntary household presence, incident acknowledgement, and incident check-in signals;
- requests, offers, request-specific messages, claims, closures, and related timestamps;
- notification, source, consent, access, security, and operator-action records needed to operate, troubleshoot, audit, and evaluate the pilot; and
- feedback I voluntarily provide.

Before information is collected, LHL will provide a pilot privacy notice describing the final data categories, purpose, visibility, operator access, retention, correction, deletion, security, and contact process. This draft does not authorize collection before those rules are approved and implemented.

LHL will not sell pilot participant data, use it for targeted advertising, or intentionally reuse it for an unrelated purpose.

LHL intends to keep participant information on participants' devices where practical and does not intend to build a centralized participant-data warehouse for the pilot. Some minimum server-side processing may still be required for source polling, push delivery, enforceable time-bounded sharing, pilot suspension, and security or audit records. The final privacy notice will state what is stored locally or centrally, how it is protected, and what is deleted after the pilot. Centrally stored pilot participant data will be deleted after documented pilot closeout unless a specific minimum record must temporarily be retained for security, incident review, legal obligations, or pilot integrity.

## Visibility and limited sharing

I understand that approved Boulder Heights members may see the group-visible profile, contact actions, requests, offers, and voluntary household signals defined in the final privacy notice. A native phone or messaging application may reveal my phone number when another approved member uses a Call or Text action.

Sensitive household assistance information is private by default. If I deliberately share it for a claimed request, access is limited to me and the authorized helper, defaults to one day, ends automatically, and may be revoked earlier. Expiration or revocation removes the helper's access; it does not by itself delete the information stored in my profile.

I will not copy, disclose, or use another participant's private or sensitive information except as reasonably needed for the specific assistance they authorized. I will not use pilot access to monitor, harass, exploit, or endanger another person.

## Withdrawal and access

I may withdraw by contacting **john@leehilllabs.com**. LHL will revoke my pilot and group access as soon as reasonably practical. Withdrawal does not recall information already disclosed to another participant or automatically erase records that must temporarily be retained for security, incident review, legal obligations, or pilot integrity.

The final privacy notice will explain how I can request access to, correction of, or deletion of my information; what is deleted, de-identified, or retained; why; and for how long. LHL may revoke access for mistaken admission or a safety or privacy concern.
